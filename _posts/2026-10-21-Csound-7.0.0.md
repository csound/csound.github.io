We are thrilled to announce the official release of **Csound 7.0.0**, a major milestone in the evolution of the Csound sound and music computing system!

Csound 7 brings deep language modernizations, a cleaner and more expressive syntax, a refactored host API, and expanded platform support while preserving backward compatibility for existing Csound orchestras and scores.

### Highlights in Csound 7.0

- **Explicit Variable Typing & Flexible Naming**: Variables are no longer restricted to single-letter prefixes (`i`, `k`, `a`) to determine their rate. You can now use explicit colon type annotations such as `freq:i = 440` or `sig:a = vco2(0.2, freq)`.
- **User-Defined Structs & Struct Arrays**: Define custom `struct` data types to group parameters and pass structured data cleanly across instruments and opcodes.
- **Modernized User-Defined Opcodes (UDOs)**: Define UDOs with function-style signatures `opcode MyFilter(in:a, cf:k):(a)` and pass-by-reference semantics.
- **Generalized Loops & Conditionals**: Enhanced `for` loops, boolean constants, and boolean functions inside conditional expressions.
- **Refactored Host & UGen APIs**: A modernized C/C++ API, PEP 8 compliant Python bindings (`ctcsound`), per-k-cycle debugger callbacks in WebAssembly, and a new UGen API.
- **Desktop, Mobile, Web & Embedded Builds**: Official release packages for macOS, Windows (x64, ARM64, i386), Linux, Android, iOS, plus embedded targets including **ESP32 (S3/P4)** and **ARM Cortex-M7**.
