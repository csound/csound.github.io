The first CsoundUnity 4.0 prerelease brings the Csound 7 beta to Unity. Use Unity Timeline to sequence Csound notes and automate parameters, connect Csound instances in an interactive audio routing graph, and capture audio through low-latency native inputs. This prerelease also adds two audio output paths for Unity 6.

CsoundUnity 4.0 requires Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer. See the [alpha.1 release notes](https://github.com/rorywalsh/CsoundUnity/releases/tag/v4.0.0-alpha.1) for the full list of changes.
