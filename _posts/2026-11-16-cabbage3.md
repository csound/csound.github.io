The next evolution of the open-source audio framework is now live and ready for download. Built from the ground up for modern sound design and plugin development, Cabbage 3 brings a streamlined workflow, expanded cross-platform capabilities, and a redesigned engine to help you turn Csound code into standalone instruments and DAW plugins faster than ever.

What’s New:

* Rebuilt Core: Improved performance, stability, and lower latency across macOS, Windows, and Linux.. 

* Modernized UI Engine: More flexible interface design tools for crafting responsive, custom plugin GUIs.

* Streamlined Workflow: Now ships directly as VS Code and Chromium extensions, allowing you to edit, test, and preview your Csound instruments right inside your existing development environment.

* AI-Ready by Design: Engineered for seamless AI-assisted development, complete with a full suite of LLM-ready documentation at cabbageaudio.com/llms.txt so you can prototype and troubleshoot faster with your favorite coding assistants, without burning endless tokens on discovery tasks.

Ready to start building? Explore the new features, browse the updated documentation, and download the latest release at http://cabbageaudio.com. Join the community discussion, share what you're making, or get support over on the forum at http://forum.cabbageaudio.com.
