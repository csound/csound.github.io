Following his presentation at ICSC 2026, Hlöðver Sigurðsson has released
[Make A Lisp for Csound 0.3.0](https://github.com/hlolli/csound-make-a-lisp/releases/tag/v0.3.0),
a Clojure-style Lisp interpreter written in Csound 7.

The [Make A Lisp Showcase](https://ide.csound.com/editor/CaGowm3UM0jY3THb1Ol9)
lets you try it in your browser through ten short, commented lessons covering
values, collections, functions, immutable data, atoms and macros. Press Play
and follow the output in the Console.

[Interactive Lisp Repl](https://ide.csound.com/editor/nzI9QU6wheOzfbFRusbN)
uses Csound's `readline` opcode. Press Play, then enter Lisp at the `lisp>`
prompt in the Console. Try `(+ 2 3)`. Definitions stay between inputs, so you
can define a function or change an atom and use it in the next expression.

Both projects include a README and the interpreter.
The [source code](https://github.com/hlolli/csound-make-a-lisp) and
[0.3.0 release](https://github.com/hlolli/csound-make-a-lisp/releases/tag/v0.3.0)
are available on GitHub.
