CsoundUnity 4.0.0-alpha.2 upgrades the bundled engine from the Csound 7 beta to the final Csound 7.0.0 release. Timeline sequencers gain tempo automation, a shared beat grid, and clip presets. Unity and Csound can now exchange arrays and spectral data, and CsoundUnity adds support for Cabbage 3 widget definitions, procedural Doppler, and recording Unity's mixed output.

Try the Master Clock, Doppler Flyby, or Diagnostics samples in Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer. See the [alpha.2 release notes](https://github.com/rorywalsh/CsoundUnity/releases/tag/v4.0.0-alpha.2) for installation and upgrade instructions.
