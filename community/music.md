From pioneering electroacoustic works at MIT and IRCAM to contemporary modular synthesis, algorithmic microtonal music, and live-coded performances, Csound has powered four decades of musical creation.

- **[Artist Portraits & Interviews](/people.html)** — Watch video interviews with composers and instrument builders discussing how Csound shapes their musical voice.
- **[Interactive Browser Examples](/examples.html)** — Listen to generative Csound pieces synthesized in real time directly in your browser.
- **[Projects & Instruments Showcase](/projects.html)** — Discover telematic performances, synthesizers, and sound installations powered by Csound.
