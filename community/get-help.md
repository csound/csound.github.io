### Mailing Lists & Frontend Communities

* **[Csound Users Mailing List (LISTSERV)](https://listserv.heanet.ie/cgi-bin/wa?A0=CSOUND)** — The long-running Csound community email list.
* **[Csound Developers Mailing List](https://listserv.heanet.ie/cgi-bin/wa?A0=CSOUND-DEV)** — Core engine architecture, APIs, and development discussions.
* **[Cabbage Audio Forum](https://forum.cabbageaudio.com)** — Dedicated forum for Cabbage plugin development.
* **[CsoundQt Mailing List](https://lists.sourceforge.net/lists/listinfo/qutecsound-users)** — Support and announcements for CsoundQt.
* **[Blue Users Mailing List](https://lists.sourceforge.net/lists/listinfo/bluemusic-users)** — Support and discussions for the Blue composition environment.
* **[Stack Overflow (`#csound`)](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/csound)** — Q&A archive tagged with `csound`.
